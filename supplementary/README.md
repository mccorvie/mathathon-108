# Files cited by the report

Copies of files of the team's working repository that the report cites as `evidence/...`: the written proofs behind the
label WRITTEN PROOF and their reviews, the statement comparisons, the computed counts and logs, the catalogue of
simplifications. In every copy the names of team members, their repository handles and the names of their machines are
replaced by bracketed text (`[team member]`, `[team handle]`, `[team machine]`); nothing else is changed. These are working
notes written by AI agents under the team's direction; the report is the record of what is claimed. The interval computations
are in `../computations/`. The Lean project is the repository itself.

Not copied here: Lean project folders that the report names under `evidence/` (the project of record is this repository; earlier projects stay in the team's working repository), the logs of AI use and the chat transcripts (they stay in the working repository), and the team's map of A. Casper's private archive. Lean statement sheets among the cited files are stored with the extension `.lean.txt`, so that they are not taken for sources of the project.
